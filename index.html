How the business is built

Three layers: from scratch to deployment

Layer 1 · Infrastructure

Data center development and deployment

Find the site, check power and cooling, design the modular data center, choose suppliers, then integrate and deploy.

Layer 2 · Hardware & supply chain

GPU and server sourcing and integration

Source GPUs, negotiate with suppliers, buy the equipment and deliver complete GPU solutions to customers.

Layer 3 · Platform & recurring revenue

GSN, the Global Supercomputing Network

Connects GPU and server owners with customers who need compute; handles allocation, provisioning, billing and monitoring.

Initiative 1 · Site by site

Why each site scores the way it does

Ishikari: cold, green, subsidised

30/35Wave 1

Power

Strong

Wind and solar on Ishikari Bay; a 100% renewable data center by Tokyu Land and Hokkaido Electric is due in 2026.

Water and cooling

Strong

Cold climate: outside air can cool servers for much of the year, which cuts energy bills.

Fiber

Watch

Far from Tokyo users; Ishikari RE Data Center signed an MOU with NeutraDC to link Hokkaido, Singapore and Indonesia.

Land

Strong

Industrial land at Ishikari Bay New Port; foreign companies can own land in Japan.

Incentives

Strong

METI subsidised SoftBank’s Tomakomai AI data center with up to ¥30bn; Hokkaido was shortlisted for data center clusters (Apr 2026).

Demand

Strong

Sakura Internet forecasts GPU revenue up 126% to ¥18.4bn; 11 companies formed the Ishikari consortium (Aug 2026).

What this means for NXCOMP

Japan wants data centers outside Tokyo, where new projects can wait years for grid power. Hokkaido has spare clean power and cold air that cuts cooling costs. NXCOMP already builds in Japan, so it can offer container data centers that go live in months; Sakura Internet already buys them.

Ho Chi Minh City: the digital hub

28/35Wave 1

Power

Watch

Occasional shortages today. The Ninh Thuan 1 nuclear plant (2,400 MW, target around 2030) will strengthen southern supply.

Water and cooling

Watch

River and industrial-park water supply; the hot climate needs efficient cooling.

Fiber

Strong

Most international cables land at Vung Tau, now part of the city; under 50 ms to Singapore.

Land

Strong

Industrial parks such as Tan Phu Trung and VSIP; foreign firms can lease up to 70 years in parks.

Incentives

Strong

100% foreign-owned data centers allowed; a city task force is speeding up a $2bn AI data center.

Demand

Strong

About half of Vietnam’s data center capacity; Viettel 140 MW, CMC, KBC; Google and Alibaba reportedly by 2027.

What this means for NXCOMP

Customers and undersea cables meet in Ho Chi Minh City, but it has only about 33 MW running and big campuses take years. NXCOMP can fill the gap with container data centers that open in months inside existing industrial parks, then grow on nuclear power after 2030.

Thailand EEC: busy, and hungry

26/35Wave 2

Power

Strong

Power producers sit inside data center joint ventures (Gulf in GSA); the BOI ties approvals to power readiness.

Water and cooling

Watch

Piped supply in industrial estates; plan ahead for the dry season.

Fiber

Strong

Next to Bangkok, in carrier-neutral industrial estates.

Land

Strong

Foreign firms can own land with BOI or IEAT approval; WHA and Amata already host data centers.

Incentives

Strong

Top BOI tier for GPU-ready data centers with PUE of 1.3 or better.

Demand

Strong

36 data center applications worth THB 728bn in 2025; True 223 MW and GSA 120 MW approved.

What this means for NXCOMP

Big players like True and Gulf are already building large data halls here, so NXCOMP should not compete to build another one. Instead, it can sell what they need inside those halls: GPU-ready modules that meet the BOI energy rules, GPUs, and the DCIM software that runs them.

Bac Ninh: AI beside the factories

25/35Wave 2

Power

Risk

The northern grid has run short before; each substation must be checked before quoting.

Water and cooling

Strong

Red River delta; piped water supply in industrial parks.

Fiber

Strong

About 30 km from Hanoi, on Viettel, VNPT and FPT networks.

Land

Strong

39 industrial parks; about 600 ha ready now and about 1,000 ha by early 2027.

Incentives

Strong

100% foreign ownership of data centers allowed; a 270 ha IT park is planned.

Demand

Strong

Samsung (about $11bn) and Amkor ($1.6bn chip plant) nearby; Vietnam has only 10,000–15,000 GPUs.

What this means for NXCOMP

Bac Ninh’s electronics factories are starting to use AI on their production lines, for example cameras that spot defects. NXCOMP can place a small container data center inside the industrial park, so factories get AI computing next door. We would sell through the park developer, who already knows every factory.

Initiative 4 · Two business models

GPU Rental vs. Token Factory

Traditional GPU Rental

Customer→Rents GPUs→Runs own models
  • Customers rent GPU capacity.
  • Customers manage their own AI models and workloads.
  • Revenue primarily based on GPU usage or reserved capacity.

AI Inference / Token Factory

NXCOMP hosts→App calls API→Pays per token
  • Platform provides GPU infrastructure, model deployment and inference APIs.
  • Customers integrate APIs into their applications.
  • Revenue based on input/output token consumption or API usage.
  • Potential for recurring, usage-based revenue.

Benchmark · What to borrow

Four lessons for Nexus Compute

From CoreWeave and Nebius

Sign contracts before building

Put reserved-capacity contracts behind every new MDC, then use them to finance the GPUs.

From Equinix and Digital Realty

Build with partners’ capital

NXCOMP develops and manages; investors fund most of the build while NXCOMP keeps a minority stake.

From Nebius Token Factory

Sell the full stack

Launch the NEXUS Token 工廠 on GSN and earn on every API call, not only on GPU hours.

From Equinix

Become the meeting point

Grow GSN into the marketplace where GPU owners and compute buyers find each other.
